Giuseppe Pasculli

Principal Pharmacometrics Scientist · London, United Kingdom

I work in model-informed drug development (MIDD), pharmacometrics, Bayesian statistics, machine learning, and quantitative clinical pharmacology.

Currently I am a Principal Pharmacometrics Scientist at Novartis, supporting evidence generation and quantitative decision making in immunology and rare diseases.

My background spans clinical pharmacy, pharmacometrics, biostatistics, data science, AI-enabled drug development, neuroscience, and regulatory research.
